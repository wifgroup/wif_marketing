Case answer

What this campaign is really about.

A new EV showroom needs local buyers who are already comparing options. The campaign had to capture that intent before it went to a competitor.

Situation

A new Simple Energy showroom in Surat needed local EV buyers, fast.

Simple Energy scooters compete on price, range and design. The showroom needed to be discoverable before buyers made a decision elsewhere.

WIF focus

High-intent search capture, local social reach and a clean inquiry path.

Google Ads targeted "electric scooter Surat" and related buying queries. Meta Ads ran awareness and retargeting. Landing pages were built to make test-ride booking the obvious next step.

Launch-window campaign generated local test-ride inquiries and showroom awareness.

Method

How to read this case study.

Read it as a decision framework, not a guarantee. The same approach only transfers when the market, offer, sales process and data quality are comparable.

  • Identify the primary buyer intent: "electric scooter near me", price comparison, test-ride booking.
  • Match the ad creative and landing page offer to that specific intent — not a generic brand page.
  • Set up lead routing so test-ride inquiries reach the showroom team while the lead is still active.
  • Review lead quality weekly: source, interest level, outcome — then adjust spend to protect what is converting.
