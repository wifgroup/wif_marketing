Where Sydney accounts leak spend

Most Sydney accounts are targeting a geography the business cannot actually serve.

The recurring failure in Sydney is not keyword selection. It is a radius drawn on a map without reference to how long it takes to cross the city, combined with campaigns that treat a CBD searcher and a western suburbs searcher as the same person with the same intent. WIF works through the geography before touching bids. This page sits under the national Google Ads Australia overview.

Radius reality

A radius on a map is not a radius in traffic.

A twenty-kilometre circle drawn around a Sydney business looks reasonable until you drive it. Crossing the harbour at the wrong hour, or moving between the inner west and the northern beaches, can take longer than a trip between suburbs twice the distance apart elsewhere. Radius targeting drawn in kilometres routinely captures enquiries that are commercially unviable once travel time is counted. WIF sets service areas by how far the team will genuinely travel for a job, which is often an irregular shape rather than a circle.

Suburb targeting

Suburb-level location layers beat one metro-wide setting.

Sydney's suburbs vary sharply in competition, property type, household profile and the kind of work being commissioned. Grouping them into a single metro target hides all of it and lets the cheapest areas absorb the budget. WIF layers location targeting so performance can be read by area, then adjusts bids or splits campaigns where the difference in lead quality justifies the extra structure.

CBD versus suburban intent

The same keyword means different things in the CBD and the suburbs.

A search from the CBD often comes from someone at work, researching on behalf of a business, comparing options, and likely to convert through a form or a callback rather than immediately. The same query from a residential suburb is more likely to be a homeowner with an immediate problem who wants to call someone now. Ad copy, extensions, landing page and even scheduling should reflect which of those the campaign is talking to, rather than serving one message to both.

Competitive verticals

Sydney's most contested categories punish loose targeting hardest.

Legal, financial services, property, trades, healthcare and B2B professional services are among the most heavily contested categories in the Sydney auction, with established advertisers and correspondingly high click costs. In those verticals a broad match term left unchecked or an untended search-term report becomes expensive quickly. The margin for sloppiness is smaller than in less competitive markets, which is why WIF prioritises negative keyword coverage and query review before anything else.

Conversion tracking

Reported conversions and actual Sydney sales rarely match.

WIF validates which conversion events are firing, whether they represent genuine buyer actions and whether the data matches CRM reality before deciding on budget direction. Where the business takes enquiries by phone, calls are tracked separately from form fills so bidding is not pushed toward whichever is simply easier to generate.

Landing page fit

Sydney buyers look for evidence you actually work in their area.

A page that claims to serve all of Sydney reads as less credible than one naming the areas the business genuinely covers. WIF reviews offer clarity, proof, page speed, form friction and message match between ad and landing page, and pushes for service-area specificity rather than metro-wide claims.

Engagement process

How WIF starts a Sydney Google Ads engagement.

The audit starts with a map rather than a keyword list. Until the serviceable area is defined honestly, every other decision in the account is being made on a false assumption about who is worth reaching.

  • Define the genuine service area by travel time, not by kilometres on a map.
  • Audit current location settings, including whether presence or interest targeting is in use.
  • Separate CBD and suburban intent where the buyer and the conversion path differ.
  • Review search terms, match types and negative coverage against Sydney's competitive verticals.
  • Validate conversion events and separate call tracking from form submissions.
  • Review landing pages for service-area specificity and message match.
  • Set a weekly review cadence with clear cost, lead quality and pipeline signals.
